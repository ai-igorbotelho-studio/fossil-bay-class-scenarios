    How it’s worked out

    The sums behind each card

    School days = days a week × teaching weeks.

    Running cost = school days × (teaching staff + classroom running cost) + classroom fixed cost + other fixed costs.

    Fee per child is the full-year fee. With “fees scale” ticked, it is reduced in proportion to school days against a five-day, usual-holidays year.

    Result = children × fee per child − running cost.

    Break-even is the smallest whole number of children whose fees cover the running cost. Donations, grants and Ministry funding are left out, so the gap shown is what the appeal and new enrolments would need to close.
